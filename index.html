How a project runs

Four steps, with you involved at each one.

  1. Discuss

    We learn what your business needs and agree on scope and timeline.

  2. Design

    You review the layout and flow before we write the code.

  3. Build

    We develop and test, sharing progress as pages and features are ready.

  4. Launch and support

    We go live together and stay on for fixes and updates.
